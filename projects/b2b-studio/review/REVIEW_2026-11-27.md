## What is wrong, in order of importance

1. **Logo films and effects are one template.** Every BD clip is the same scratchy mark on black with a different material. Most are static after the first second and many effects do not show their name (origami, tattoo ink, sand, stained glass, galaxy). The header loops repeat the gallery effects.
2. **Sector films follow templates.** Commercials: one protagonist through five places and the same end card. Explainers: five disconnected stock shots (the weakest type). Transforms: a different room per shot, so there is no before/after. Recurring props (coffee cup, server rack, robot arm, padlock).
3. **Company films carry no brand.** Only the end card names the company; products, packs, vans and buildings are unmarked. Wrong place: Belgrade looks like Manhattan/London, Zito's street looks British, Viva Fresh casting looks Western.
4. **Reuse across pitches.** The nine LED makers received the same two films. Real-estate and supermarket pages share one shot list.
5. **Specific defects.** Foton: truck model, colour and badge change between shots, garbled lettering. Garbled pack/bag text (Vincinni, Zito). Telekom burned-in 'concept' watermark. End-card slogan clipped at the frame edges in four sector films (agro_5, furniture_3, horeca_2, horeca_3). Two Drugi tir films have a ghosted bridge frame. Albanian text-to-speech failed a speech check.
