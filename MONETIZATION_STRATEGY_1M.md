## 3. THREE STRATEGIC OPTIONS (framework: Three Horizons)

### OPTION A — "Content House" (Horizon 1-2 core)
Double down on owning a kids' content brand: execute the 23-episode season, run MK + EN channels, build the SVOD layer, expand languages. Monetize attention.
- *Pros:* Compounds an owned audience; leverages the entire catalog; blue-ocean MK position.
- *Cons:* Slow audience growth; platform risk; revenue is INFERRED and back-loaded.
- *Fit:* Best if the director wants a durable media brand.

### OPTION B — "AI Production Studio" (Horizon 1 cash engine)
Sell production as a service: B2B films for brands/educators/culture, personalized films, identity/LoRA builds. Monetize the factory.
- *Pros:* Revenue starts with the first client; unit economics proven; the Abracadabra menu is the reference sale.
- *Cons:* Service business scales linearly with attention; less compounding than owned IP.
- *Fit:* Fastest path to first revenue and to $1M cumulative; natural fit for an existing production operation.

### OPTION C — "Methodology & Platform" (Horizon 2-3)
Productize the system: license the KB + tooling + training; build a self-serve or semi-serve "AI children's film" platform for creators/studios.
- *Pros:* Highest margin; pure IP leverage; scales without per-unit attention.
- *Cons:* Requires audience/trust in the methodology first; longer to validate demand.
- *Fit:* Best AFTER A or B proves demand and produces public proof-of-work.
