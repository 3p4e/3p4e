## 1. Positioning and go-to-market

**Category:** AI-produced commercial video for Balkan companies, in local languages, delivered in days.

**Positioning statement:**
For marketing and HR leads at fast-growing Western Balkan companies who need a steady flow of professional video but can't justify a film crew for every launch, we make studio-grade AI films of *their* products, sites and plants in about a week, for a fraction of a crew's price, and we keep making them every month.

**Why us, not the alternatives:**

| Alternative | Their weakness | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| Local film crew / agency | €3-15K per film, weeks of scheduling, can't show what doesn't exist yet | days, a fraction of the price, can show future buildings and products |
| Global AI tools used in-house (Higgsfield, Runway, Kling) | the client has no time or skill; results inconsistent | a tested pipeline (start/end keyframes, locked camera, brand accuracy) |
| 3D render studios (real estate) | stills or expensive animation | animated timelapse from their own renders |
| Stock footage | not their product, not their building | it is their product, building or plant |

**Beachheads, in order:**
1. Construction and real-estate developers (MK first, then Serbia, Albania, Kosovo): the shortest path from demo to deal.
2. Car and truck importers/dealers (Foton pitch ready).
3. Fast-growing manufacturers and FMCG, sold through local ad agencies (white-label).
4. Non-EU-market companies as the MiniMax-H3 lane (domestic or non-EU display only).

**Channels:** in person in Skopje; LinkedIn and email to marketing/HR heads; 2-3 MK ad agencies as white-label partners; regional trade fairs (non-EU); referrals from each finished client.

## 2. Pricing strategy

Value-based, three tiers, priced against a crew/agency alternative, not against GPU cost. Prices in EUR, North Macedonia (E); Serbia similar or +10-20%; Albania/Kosovo/Montenegro/BiH similar to MK.

| Tier | What | Price | Your hours (E) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | One 20-30 s film: 4-6 shots, music, logo, 16:9 + 9:16 cut | €600-900 | 4-6 |
| **Launch** | Hero film 45-60 s + 3 short spots + 10 social cuts | €1,800-3,500 | 14-20 |
| **Monthly** | 4-8 new clips a month, adapted to campaigns | €400-900 / month, 3-month minimum | 6-10 / month |

Rules:
- **No free samples for strangers.** One free spec clip only after a qualifying meeting, and only from batched renders.
- **Anchor high:** show the crew/agency price first, then the Launch tier; Pilot exists to de-risk, not to be the main offer.
- **Usage rights in the price:** web + social + screens included; broadcast TV and paid ads at scale +30%.
- **MiniMax-H3 work:** only for domestic/non-EU display, labelled "AI-generated · Made with MiniMax H3"; offer LTX-made versions for anything shown in the EU.
- Re-price after the first 5 quotes; if everyone says yes instantly, prices are too low.

## 3. First customers plan

Goal: **3 paying clients in 45 days.**

1. **List:** 40 named prospects: 15 MK (research files), 25 from the non-EU Balkan shortlist (being researched now).
2. **Personalised hook:** for each of the top 10, a 10-15 s spec clip of *their* world (site, product, vehicle, plant), made from public imagery for the pitch only, marked "concept, not for publication".
3. **Outreach cadence:** day 0 personal message with the clip; day 3 follow-up with one more frame; day 7 call request; stop after 3 touches.
4. **Agency route in parallel:** pitch 3 MK agencies on white-label (they sell, you produce at 30-40% below your direct price).
5. **Close with the Pilot tier**, then upsell Launch/Monthly after delivery.

Expected funnel (E): 40 contacts → 12 replies → 6 meetings → 3 pilots.

## 4. Customer interviews (use in every first meeting)

Ask before pitching; listen for budget, frequency and decision process.
1. How did you produce your last 3 videos? Who made them, how long, what did it cost?
2. What video do you wish you had but haven't made? Why not?
3. Who signs off a video purchase, and from which budget?
4. How often do you need new content (launches, hiring, projects)?
5. What would make you uncomfortable about AI-made video? (brand accuracy, disclosure, quality)
6. If a film like this were ready in a week, what would you use it for first?

Signals to log: current spend per video, frequency, decision maker, objection. Re-price from these, not from guesses.

## 5. Unit economics and runway

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| GPU per LTX clip (batch) | ~$0.04 (M) |
| GPU per MiniMax-H3 clip, self-hosted 2×A100 | ~$0.30 (M: 6.8 min at $2.71/h) |
| Keyframe image (Gemini 3 Pro Image) | a few cents to tens of cents each (check billing) |
| Box setup overhead | $0.30-2 per session (M) |
| Fixed tools / hosting | €100-250 / month (E) |
| Hours per Pilot / Launch / Monthly | 4-6 / 14-20 / 6-10 (E) |
| Contribution after direct costs | ~85-90% of revenue (before your pay) |
| Break-even for a modest €1,500/month personal draw + tools | ~€1,750/month = 1 Launch, or 2 Pilots + 1 Monthly |

Runway: costs are tiny; the real runway is your time. Cap free/spec work at 20% of weekly hours until the first 3 clients pay.

## 6. North-star metric and inputs

- **North star:** paid video minutes delivered per month (captures volume and repeat business).
- **Input metrics:** qualified meetings/week (target 2), proposal→win rate (target ≥30%), hours per project (target ≤8 for Launch after templating), % revenue recurring (target 40% by month 9), client NPS / repeat rate.

## 7. Pitch outline (per prospect, 7 slides)

1. **Their moment:** their growth fact from the research (with source).
2. **The gap:** what video they lack today (from their site/social).
3. **The concept:** 3 keyframes of *their* world + the spec clip.
4. **The plan:** Launch tier contents and timeline (7-10 days).
5. **Proof:** Abracadabra board, construction timelapse reel, Foton clips.
6. **Price and rights:** tier price, usage rights, AI disclosure line.
7. **Next step:** Pilot this month.

## 8. Skeptical-investor check (stress test)

| Challenge | Honest answer | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| "Anyone can buy Higgsfield or Runway; where's the moat?" | Thin. The tools are commodities. | Vertical templates, speed, local relationships, agency channel; aim for retainers, not one-offs. |
| "Will Balkan companies pay real money for AI video?" | Unproven; zero paid clients yet. | 3 pilots in 45 days is the test; change offer if 40 contacts produce 0 pilots. |
| "Brand accuracy: will the generated product really look like theirs?" | Not always (Foton V7 first attempt was wrong). | Build keyframes from client-supplied images; approval step on keyframes before rendering. |
| "Licences and law?" | MiniMax-H3 output can't be shown in EU/UK/US/KR; EU AI Act disclosure; trademark use. | LTX for EU-facing work; written permissions; AI label in every contract. |
| "Single operator: what if you're sick or busy?" | Real risk. | Documented pipeline in the repo; first hire at 80 billable h/month. |
| "Infrastructure reliability?" | Rentals failed several times today. | Proven rent-and-verify recipe; hosted fallback (Higgsfield) for deadlines. |
| "Is $1M realistic?" | Not in year 1. | Year-1 target €34-65K; $1M needs a team and export markets (years 3-5). |
