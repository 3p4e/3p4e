# Волшебната книга — Епизода 1: „Патување во центарот на Земјата“

**Source:** two pages of the book (82–83): „Патување во центарот на Земјата“ and „Подземниот свет“, after Jules Verne. The novel is in the public domain; the narration is in our own words.
**Format:** 2D painted storybook · ages 3–6 · Macedonian narration · target length **≈ 7:10**
**Narrator:** a warm female storyteller (Gemini **Leda**). The professor's lines can use a second, deeper voice.
**Why this one first:**
- Dinosaurs, a volcano and an ocean under the ground are a big draw for ages 3–6.
- There is **no violence**: nobody is hurt and there are no villains.
- It also teaches without effort what a volcano is, and where Iceland and Italy are.
