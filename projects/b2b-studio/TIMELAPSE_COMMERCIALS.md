# Timelapse commercials (sped-up build) from the stage keyframes

Each commercial is one locked camera and one place, moving through its stages with start+end keyframes per clip, so the whole film reads as a single sped-up shot. Rendered on LTX-2.5 and queued on MiniMax-H3 (H3 versions: domestic/non-EU display only). Voice-over in the client's language; lines below are the English master.

## Voice-over masters (one line per clip)

**Construction:** "Every home starts as bare ground." / "We pour the strength it stands on." / "Floor by floor, it rises." / "Glass, light, shelter." / "The last details, done right." / "From empty plot to your keys."

**Developer:** "Today, a plot." / "Tomorrow, a skyline." / "Reserve your place before it's built."

**Apartment staging:** "Empty today." / "Furnished tomorrow." / "Home, the day you move in."

**Interior design:** "It starts with bare walls." / "Then an idea on paper." / "Then a room that feels like you."

**Renovation:** "Before." / "After. Done on time."

## Other businesses that can reuse the same method
- **Kitchen and bathroom makers** (extras `ren_x1`, `ren_x2`): old room → new kitchen/bath in one shot.
- **Furniture and home retailers:** the apartment set with their own products as the pieces that arrive.
- **Landscaping and garden firms:** bare yard → lawn, trees, paths (same technique; keyframes to be made).
- **Shop fitters and retail chains** (KAM, Viva Fresh): empty unit → fully stocked store before opening day.
- **Event and stage builders, exhibition stands:** empty hall → finished stand.
- **Solar and energy installers:** bare roof or field → panels in place.
- **Road and infrastructure contractors:** graded earth → finished road (`con`-style locked aerial).

For a real client, the stage keyframes are built from their own renders, plans or site photos, so the film shows their actual project.
