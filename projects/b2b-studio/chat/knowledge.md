## FAQ
Q: What is AI video?
A: Video where the shots are not filmed with a camera but generated with artificial intelligence models. We write the script, create the key frames, animate them, add voice-over, music and subtitles, and edit a finished film with your brand.
Q: Do we need to film anything?
A: No. An idea, a few photos or renders, your logo and a short explanation are enough. If you already have photos of a product or site, we use them as the starting point.
Q: How long does it take?
A: A pilot film is ready 7–10 days after you approve the key frames. For the Launch package and monthly content we agree the timeline based on the scope.
Q: Can we use our own products and logo?
A: Yes. Your logo, colours and end card are part of every film, and we build your products from your own photos. You approve every key frame before animation, so only what you have signed off goes on screen.
Q: Who owns the rights, and is the video labelled as AI?
A: You get usage rights for web, social media and screens; TV and paid campaigns by agreement. Every video is clearly labelled as made with artificial intelligence, as the platforms (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) also require.
Q: Which languages do you work in?
A: Voice-over and subtitles in Macedonian, Serbian and Albanian, for markets in North Macedonia, Serbia, Albania and Kosovo. English subtitles on request.
