# BIG DOGMA outreach kit

One file per company (`01_zito_marketi_MK.md` … `12_speeex_XK.md`), in the company's language: cold email, LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters), WhatsApp message, two follow-ups. Tracker: `outreach_tracker.xlsx` (follow-up dates fill in automatically from the "Day 0 sent" date; status has a drop-down).

## Before the first send
1. Replace the placeholders: `[Име и презиме]` / `[Ime i prezime]` / `[Emri dhe mbiemri]`, `[Име]` / `[Ime]` / `[Emri]`, and the studio phone, email and web in the signature (still `+389 7X XXX XXX`, `studio@example.mk`, `www.example.mk`). The same placeholders are in the films' end cards, PDFs and pages: send the real details and everything is re-rendered once.
2. Find the right person: the "Who to contact" role in each file. Company website (About / Contact / Press), LinkedIn search "<company> marketing", or the reception phone asking for the marketing department. Write the name and email into the tracker.
3. Send from a real domain mailbox (not Gmail), with a short signature and no attachments on the first email (the link carries everything; attachments trigger spam filters). The offer PDF is a button on the page.

## Sequence
| Day | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email | section 1 |
| 0 | LinkedIn | section 2 (connection request with note) |
| 4 | Email reply in the same thread | section 4 |
| 10 | Email reply in the same thread | section 5 (last) |
| any | WhatsApp | section 3, only if you already have the number from a business card, the company website or a referral |

Best send times: Tuesday to Thursday, 9:00–11:00 local time. Stop the sequence the moment someone replies.

## When they reply
- Book 15 minutes (in person or online). Show the page on a laptop or TV, then the "how it's made" explainer.
- Ask for 5–10 of their own photos, renders or product shots and their logo files: the pilot uses their real material.
- Send the offer PDF from the page and the licence sheet from `DELIVERY_AND_LICENCE.md` after the call.

## Rules
- Only the private LTX pages and LTX files go out by email or link. **H3 films are never emailed, uploaded or linked**: show them in person on your laptop only (H3 licence: no use or display in the EU, UK, US or South Korea, which includes open online links).
- Each company gets only its own link. Do not forward one company's page to another.
- Do not post any concept publicly. Every page and film says it is a concept that will not be published without written approval.
- Do not message private mobile numbers you found by scraping; use business contacts only.
