# Волшебната книга — all stories from the book (v2)

**Rule:** no story is left out, and no event is removed from any story. The hard moments stay in the narration but are **not shown**: we use a cut, a silhouette or the camera turning away.
Stories split across two pages in the book (Robinson, Verne, Ivan, Mečta, Gawain) become **one episode each**, covering both pages, about 6–7 min.

**Total: 23 stories, 23 episodes.** Ordered from gentlest to most demanding, alternating cultures.
