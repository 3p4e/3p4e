# Pitch kits: LTX road (2026-11-24)

The **LTX road** and the **H3 road** are built in parallel for the same businesses. Each is finished separately, then we pick one per business before anything is sent. This file covers the LTX road. Its films may be shown anywhere, including the EU, but only as a private pitch until the business approves in writing.

Every film carries the end card: the "concept only, not published without written approval" disclaimer in the local language, plus an AI label. Each kit folder (`PITCH_KITS_LTX.zip`) contains:

- the 16:9 film(s)
- the 9:16 vertical version(s)
- `message.txt`, the outreach message below

Placeholders: **[Име / Ime / Emri]** is your name, **[Студио / Studio]** the studio name, **[телефон / telefon]** your phone number.

## Rules for every kit

- **Who to write to:** the head of marketing. For the recruitment films (Alkaloid employer film, SPEEEX, Millennium Team) write to the HR / employer-branding lead. Use LinkedIn or the company's contact form, never a generic info@ address if a person can be found.
- **Cadence:** day 0 message with the film; day 3 one follow-up with a single still; day 7 ask for a 15-minute call; stop after 3 touches.
- **Offer:**

  | Tier | Content | Price |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pilot | one 20–30 s film, 16:9 + 9:16 | €600–900 |
  | Launch | hero film + 3 spots + 10 social cuts | €1,800–3,500 |
  | Monthly | | €400–900 |

  Serbia +10–20%. Albania and Kosovo as North Macedonia.
- **Native check:** a native speaker should read the Serbian and Albanian messages before sending.
