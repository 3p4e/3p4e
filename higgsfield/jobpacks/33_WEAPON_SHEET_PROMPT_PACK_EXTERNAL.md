# WEAPON SHEET PROMPT PACK — for ANY image platform (Gemini / Grok / Midjourney / Qwen / Flux)
# Both weapons are LEGENDARY GRADE. One weapon per sheet. Grey studio background.
# Output format is identical for both so sheets can sit side by side.

## OUTPUT FORMAT (mandatory, append to every prompt)
"Produce ONE single image: an orthographic multi-view weapon reference
sheet on a plain neutral grey studio background, soft even lighting, no
shadows except a soft contact shadow, weapon rendered full-frame and
centered in each view, consistent scale across views, no hands, no
person, no text, no watermark, no logo, no frame borders. Views arranged
in a 2x3 grid: (1) front three-quarter view, (2) side profile, (3) top
view, (4) rear three-quarter view, (5) detail close-up of the weapon
head/limb, (6) detail close-up of the grip/hilt."

## WEAPON 1 — DD's twin hand crossbows, legendary grade
PROMPT:
"Legendary fantasy HAND CROSSBOW weapon reference sheet: a matched TWIN
PAIR of compact hand crossbows built for a demon hunter. Pistol-grip
hand crossbows with a curved bow limb of translucent ice-blue arcane
crystal, the crystal limb glowing teal-blue from within; a slender dark
steel body with engraved frost runes running along it; the bowstring is
a line of frozen pale-blue light; faint frost mist curls off the limbs;
matching pair, mirror-imaged left and right. FANTASY DESIGN — this is a
medieval-fantasy hand crossbow: NO barrel, NO magazine, NO gun parts, NO
trigger guard like a firearm, NO Uzi or machinegun silhouette; it is
strung with a bowstring and fires bolts like a crossbow. Style: Diablo
legendary item concept art, high-detail game-cinematic quality,
photoreal materials (crystal, steel, frost)."
ANTI-SLOP NEGATIVE (append if the platform supports negatives):
"firearm, gun, machinegun, Uzi, SMG, barrel, magazine, ammo clip, muzzle,
stock, modern weapon"

## WEAPON 2 — Azulon's two-handed greatsword, legendary grade
PROMPT:
"Legendary fantasy TWO-HANDED GREATSWORD weapon reference sheet: a

the blade, the edge wreathed in soft golden holy fire; a sunburst

ANTI-SLOP NEGATIVE:

## USAGE
Feed each prompt to the external platform as-is. Each returns one sheet.
The sheets become the locked weapon Elements in the VAST pipeline
(image1 slot per the anchor policy) and are also your reference when
judging whether any render's weapon matches.
