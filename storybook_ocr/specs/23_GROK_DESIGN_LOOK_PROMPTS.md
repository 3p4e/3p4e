## DESIGN GUARDS (canon — carry into every regeneration)

1. **Perseus:** AZ's exact face (clean-shaven, retired-paladin bone structure). Bronze Greek hoplite armour, short curly dark hair, winged sandals, adamantine sword (harpe), mirrored bronze shield. No beard, no modern gear.
2. **Medusa:** Feminized DD face — beautiful but terrifying. Snakes for hair (never a wig), golden reptilian slit-pupil eyes, bronze scaled skin on shoulders/arms, bat-like wings folded on back, lower body snake-tail (not human legs). No blood, no gore.
3. **Athena:** Tall, serene, silver-bronze Corinthian helmet (face visible), spear (Aegis), owl motif. No cross-pollination with Medusa's snake motifs.
4. **Hermes:** Young, quick, winged sandals (talaria), winged golden helmet (petasos), caduceus. No beard, no armour plates.
5. **No gore / no decapitation shown.** Medusa's defeat is implied by a fall, not a severed head.
6. **Palette separation:** Perseus = bronze/white/sea-blue; Medusa = dark green/bronze/black; Athena = silver/gold/white; Hermes = gold/white/sky; Pegasus = pure white/gold; Andromeda = white/sea-green; King = purple/gold.
