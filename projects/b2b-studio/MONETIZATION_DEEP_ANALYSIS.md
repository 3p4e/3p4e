# Monetization deep analysis: all fields (2026-11-23)

Scope: every revenue field in the project: the B2B studio verticals (F&B menu boards, industrial/manufacturing, construction/real estate/interior, car dealers), the SMB mascot retainer, wedding myth films, kids' content, methodology licensing, identity/LoRA service, regional expansion and clip licensing.

Method (founder frameworks): problem/willingness-to-pay per segment, unit economics **per hour of your time** (the binding constraint), sales cycle and funnel math, competition and moat, risks, a capacity-bound 12-month model, and kill/scale gates. Inputs: measured costs from this project, the market research in `B2B_RESEARCH_AND_PROMPTS.md`, and `MONETIZATION_REVIEW.md`.

Labels: **M** measured, **E** estimate to validate with the first quotes, **V** verify before relying on it. All prices in EUR for North Macedonia unless stated.

---

## 1. The one number that matters: revenue per hour

GPU and image API costs are 1-3% of any price (M: ~$0.04 per clip in batch, ~$1-2 GPU per 12-clip batch including setup). The business is limited by **your hours**: brief, keyframe rounds, QC, edit, music, revisions, delivery, plus selling. So rank fields by euros earned per hour of work.

| Field | Typical deal (E) | Delivery hours (E) | € per delivery hour | Sales cycle (E) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial / manufacturer brand or recruitment film | €3,000 | 10-14 h | **€215-300** | 2-6 months, committee |
| Construction timelapse film (8 stages) | €1,700 | 7-9 h | **€190-240** | 1-3 months |
| Car dealer launch pack (film + 3 spots + 10 cuts) | €2,500 | 14-18 h | **€140-180** | 1-2 months |
| Car dealer monthly content | €600/mo | 7-9 h/mo | €65-85 | after launch pack |
| Apartment staging package (3-5 layouts) | €1,400 | 10-14 h | €100-140 | 1-2 months |
| Menu board setup (Abracadabra type) | €1,000 | 14-18 h | €55-70 | 2-4 weeks |
| Menu board monthly | €70/mo | ~1 h/mo | €70 | recurring |
| SMB mascot retainer | €180/mo | 3-4 h/mo | €45-60 | 2-4 weeks, churn risk |
| Wedding myth film (MK) | €250 | 8-12 h | **€20-30** | event-driven, consumer support load |
| Kids' YouTube episode | €0-30 ad revenue in year 1 (E) | 12-20 h | **~€1-3** | audience building, 12+ months |

**Conclusion:** B2B project work (industrial, construction, dealers) earns 3-10x more per hour than SMB retainers or consumer products in North Macedonia. Kids' content does not pay for its time in year 1.

## 2. Field-by-field assessment

### 2.1 Industrial / manufacturing giants (Alkaloid, Makstil, Usje, Tikves, Zito Luks, automotive FDI plants)
- **Pain:** employer branding (hiring is hard for the FDI plants: Kromberg & Schubert ~6,900 staff, Draexlmaier 6,000+, Adient 1,400), export trade-fair and investor material, anniversaries (Alkaloid's 90th used a Croatian agency, More Magnets, per press, ⚠️).
- **Willingness to pay:** highest in the country, but decisions often sit at HQ (FDI) or with an existing agency.
- **Channel:** direct to communications/HR heads where a named contact exists (OKTA publishes one), and **white-label through MK agencies** that already hold these accounts (fastest route; they resell, you produce).
- **Risks:** long cycles; real-plant accuracy expectations (they will want their own footage mixed in); brand/trademark use needs their approval; AI disclosure in EU-facing material (EU AI Act transparency rules from Aug 2026, V).
- **Verdict:** **Primary target**, but use agencies as the channel and expect 2-6 months before the first deal.

### 2.2 Construction, developers, interior studios (Granit, Adora, Limak, East Gate, Stenton, Zikol, Krmzov-MR, Turmak; Atmosly, AMATA, KL Studio, Krug, GVRL+)
- **Pain:** selling apartments off-plan needs visuals before the building exists; drone footage is impossible before construction; 3D render studios charge per still.
- **Product fit:** strongest of all fields: the stage-keyframe method shows a future building growing and an empty flat furnishing itself, which is exactly the pre-sales story. The demo is rendering now.
- **Willingness to pay:** good; developers already pay for renders and brochures.
- **Accuracy risk:** clients will want *their* building. Offer: client supplies architect renders or plans; keyframes are made from those (image edit), so the video matches the project.
- **Verdict:** **Lead vertical**: shortest path from demo to paid deal.

### 2.3 Car dealers (Foton Macedonia first, then other importers)
- **Pain:** a recent brand push (site built 2026), one CTA (test drive), two audiences (pickups, fleet/trucks).
- **Product:** launch pack + monthly social content; fits a retainer.
- **Risks:** vehicle accuracy (generated pickups must match real models: V9 matched well, V7 and trucks did not); brand permission from dealer and possibly the manufacturer.
- **Verdict:** **Strong secondary**: one dealer win is replicable across the ~30+ importers in Skopje.

### 2.4 F&B menu boards (Abracadabra)
- **Proof:** live product with 126 clips, two screens, old-TV USB variant (M).
- **Economics:** decent setup fee, small recurring fee, ~15 h setup per venue unless templated.
- **Lever:** template the board + clip pipeline so a new venue takes ≤6 h; then €1,000 / 6 h = €165/h.
- **Verdict:** **Keep as a productized side line**; good proof and local referrals, not the main income.

### 2.5 SMB mascot retainer
- **Economics:** €45-60/h in MK, churn risk, many small clients to manage.
- **Verdict:** **Do not lead with it in MK.** Revisit for diaspora/EU clients at EU prices once a portfolio exists.

### 2.6 Wedding myth films (consumer)
- **Economics:** €20-30/h in MK, high emotional support load, likeness consent and refunds.
- **Verdict:** **Park.** Only viable at EU prices (€300-700) with an automated intake; not a year-1 field.

### 2.7 Kids' content (YouTube AVOD/SVOD, 23-episode season)
- **Economics:** made-for-kids ads pay little; discovery takes a year+; YouTube policy targets mass-produced/repetitive content (V).
- **Verdict:** **Brand/portfolio project, not income.** Fund it from B2B profit, cap time at ~10%.

### 2.8 Methodology course / licensing, identity-LoRA service, clip licensing, regional language expansion
- **Course/licensing:** needs public proof and an audience first; pre-sell before building. Year 2.
- **Identity/LoRA service:** consent, biometrics and refund risk; B2B only (brand ambassadors with contracts). Year 2.
- **Clip licensing / shorts:** small amounts; do it opportunistically with existing renders.
- **Regional expansion (Serbia, Bulgaria, Albania, Kosovo):** same B2B playbook after MK works; language and local presence needed.

## 3. Competition and moat (honest)

- **Competitors:** MK ad agencies and video crews adopting Higgsfield, Runway, Kling, Veo and similar tools; freelancers; 3D render studios for real estate.
- **Your edge today:** a working, cheap, repeatable pipeline (start-frame keyframes, consistent locations and characters), local language and presence, a live reference client, very low cost per clip.
- **Moat is thin and shrinking:** tools get easier every quarter, so per-clip price will fall. Defend with: vertical templates (construction, dealer, industrial), speed (days, not weeks), account relationships, and owning the agency channel. Expect to cut prices 20-40% over 18-24 months as competitors adopt the same tools.

## 4. Sales funnel math (E)

B2B in MK: 15 qualified contacts → 5 meetings → 3 proposals → **1 win** (~7%).
- 2 wins/month needs **~30 qualified contacts/month** (about 7 a week).
- Agency white-label: 1 agency partner can bring 1-3 projects/quarter with no direct selling.
- Free sample policy: only after a meeting, and batch samples (5-10 per rental) to keep cost ~€0.30 each.

## 5. Capacity-bound 12-month model (single operator)

Assumptions (E): ~100 billable hours/month maximum, ~40 h/month selling; average B2B project €1,800 at ~10 h; dealer/retainer average €400/mo at ~6 h; menu boards €1,000 + €70/mo.

| Scenario | Projects (12 mo) | Retainers (avg active) | Menu boards | Revenue | Delivery hours/month (of ~100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 12 | 2 | 2 | **~€34K** | ~25 h |
| Base | 25 | 3-4 | 3 | **~€60-65K** | ~45 h |
| Upside (one industrial anchor + agency channel) | 35 | 5 | 4 | **~€90-100K** | ~65 h + selling: near capacity, hire soon |

Costs (E): tools and subscriptions €100-250/month (image API, model/tool subscriptions, music library, hosting), GPU €20-80/month, accounting and company costs; total ~€3-5K/year. Contribution before your own pay ≈ **85-90% of revenue**.

Hiring trigger: when billable hours exceed ~80/month for 2 months, hire a junior editor/producer (MK cost ~€700-1,100/month, E) to take QC, edit and delivery; you keep sales and creative direction.

**$1M:** needs ~400-500 projects/year at ~€2K, a team of 5-8 and clients outside MK (EU at 2-3x MK prices). Realistic in 3-5 years if the MK playbook works and is exported; not in year 1.

## 6. Sequencing (what to do, in order)

1. **Now (weeks 1-4):** finish construction + apartment demo reels; Foton pitch pack (fix V7/trucks); one industrial sample clip; price sheet; contact 15 MK prospects + 3 MK agencies (white-label offer).
2. **Months 2-3:** close 2-3 projects (construction first); template the construction and dealer flows (target ≤8 h per project); log hours on every job.
3. **Months 4-6:** second vertical repeat clients; first dealer retainer; first agency partner; re-price on evidence.
4. **Months 7-12:** hire when the trigger hits; start the regional (Serbia/Bulgaria) outreach with the portfolio; start the methodology pre-sale only if you have public proof and an audience.

## 7. Gates (kill or scale)

| Gate | By | Pass | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | day 30 | 2 paid projects or 1 retainer | change offer: price, format or vertical |
| G2 | day 90 | ≥€3K/month revenue, one repeat client | narrow to the best-converting vertical only |
| G3 | month 6 | ≥€5K/month, delivery ≤8 h/project | if hours stay high, productize before selling more |
| G4 | month 9 | ≥€8K/month for 2 months | hire; if not, stay solo and raise prices |

## 8. Risks that can stop the business

1. **Model licences:** LTX-2.5 commercial terms (V); never publish MiniMax-H3 output where its licence forbids display (see `MINIMAX` notes when that task runs).
2. **Brand/likeness permissions:** written approval before publishing any real brand, product or person.
3. **AI disclosure:** add a contract clause; label videos where EU rules require (V).
4. **Pipeline reliability:** today's rental failures cost ~$3-4 and hours; keep the proven rent-and-verify recipe and one fallback (hosted Higgsfield/Kling) for urgent client work.
5. **Single-operator dependence:** document the pipeline (already in the repo) so a hire can run it.
6. **Price erosion:** compete on vertical templates, speed and relationships, not per-clip price.

## 9. Bottom line

Lead with **construction/real estate**, then **car dealers** and **industrial via agencies**; keep **menu boards** as a templated side line; park **weddings, kids' content, courses and identity services** until B2B pays the bills. Realistic year 1: **~€34K conservative, ~€60-65K base, ~€90-100K upside**, at very high contribution margin. The $1M goal is a team-and-export outcome in years 3-5, reached by repeating one vertical playbook, not by running all fields at once.
