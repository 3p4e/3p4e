# 16 — THE ORIGAMI ASCENSION (v3) — HERO vs VILLAIN FIGHT

Supersedes `15_ORIGAMI_ASCENSION_v2_CANON.md` (v2).
v2 fixed *who* the villain is. **v3 makes it a fight.**

---

## CANON (unchanged from v2)

**Azulon — the sent seraph.** Hero. Face-locked to `A_bust.png` + `sheet_front.png`.
Clean ivory folded parchment from the neck down, six immaculate wings, every feather
edge burning with a cold white flame that does not consume the paper. Real human face,
clean-shaven, long high-elf ears, Selûne crescent on the forehead.

**The Corrupted Seraph — "the Stain."** Villain. Parchment like Azulon, but rotted from
the wing edges inward. Black glossy tar-goo drips from every wing tip in long strands,
pools beneath it, soaks dark tide-lines along the folds, smears the blackletter script
printed on the paper. Its head is a **blank folded mask with no human face**, eye-holes
weeping black. Too many wing joints. It is a mirror of Azulon with the light taken out.

---

## THE RULE OF THIS FIGHT

They are the **same material** — folded paper. So the fight is not fire vs fire.
It is **the same weapon turned against itself**:

| | Azulon | The Stain |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | folds — **compresses**, creates edges | unfolds — **expands**, creates mass |
| Element | cold white flame (does not consume) | black wet tar (does consume) |
| Motion | precise, one clean line | overwhelming, floods the frame |
| Scale grows by | becoming a **blade** | becoming a **cathedral** |

Every attack must **release vast force** — shockwaves, light blooms, sheets of paper
shredded into confetti, goo vaporising, the void itself bending. No small gestures.

---

## ACT 1 — THE STAIN (beats 1–2)

**01 · CORRUPTION REVEALED — MONSTER**
Wide low angle. The Stain looms over an invisible floor. Six ragged wings, edges black
and dripping, glossy tar falling in long strands and pooling in a wide dark mirror.
Its blank mask tilts down, eye-holes weeping black. The void around it is stained.

**02 · THE HUNTER DESCENDS — HERO**
Azulon drops through the void, six ivory wings folded tight — then they **snap open**
and cold white flame races along every edge at once. Folded pages spiral up around his
raised hand. Far below, the Stain's silhouette drips in the dark. He does not slow.

---

## ACT 2 — FIRST EXCHANGE (beats 3–6)

**03 · THOUSAND CRANES — HERO**
His wings **fragment**. Thousands of folded paper cranes tear free, each edged in cold
white fire, streaking away in a comet swarm that fills the frame with motion streaks
and burning feather-trails. Vast release: the swarm is so dense it lights the void.

**04 · THE STAIN UNFOLDS AND DEVOURS — MONSTER**
It does not dodge. Its wings **unfold** into a cathedral wall of stained parchment.
The crane swarm strikes it and is swallowed — white fire drowning in black goo, the
goo surging brighter as it eats the light. Tentacles of tar lash out and catch the
last cranes mid-flight. It grows.

**05 · THE TAR TIDE — BOTH**
Its answer: a **tidal wave of black tar** erupts from every wing tip, an ocean of glossy
goo surging across the void. Azulon stands tiny and white against a wall of black
taller than a building, his flame reflected in the oncoming slick.

**06 · THE BLADE SPLITS THE TIDE — HERO**
Azulon re-folds himself into a **single blade** of folded paper and dives. The tar wave
parts around him in a canyon, edges burning cold white, the goo screaming into steam
and vapour. He cuts the whole ocean in half and comes out the far side.

---

## ACT 3 — ESCALATION (beats 7–9)

**07 · COLOSSUS AGAINST CATHEDRAL — BOTH**
Both stop holding size. Azulon re-folds into a **towering colossus** of angular ivory
plates burning white. The Stain unfolds into an **ever-growing mass** of stained pages
that fills the frame edge to edge. The void is now crowded with both of them.

**08 · THE SPHERE OF ANNIHILATION — BOTH**
They collide. White flame and black tar meet in a **sphere of annihilation** at frame
centre — a blinding core, concentric shockwaves shredding floating pages into confetti,
lens bloom, the void itself bowing outward under the force. The largest release yet.

**09 · THOUSANDFOLD DAWN — HERO**
Azulon's ultimate. All six wings **detonate outward** into uncountable paper blades
edged in cold white fire — a hurricane of light radiating from his body, thousands of
burning edges spiralling outward in every direction at once.

---

## ACT 4 — KILL (beats 10–12)

**10 · THE FINAL UNFOLD — MONSTER**
Cornered, the Stain **unfolds completely** — a cathedral-sized nest of stained pages and
tar tentacles, a grotesque blooming flower of rot, eating the blade storm as fast as it
arrives, swelling beyond the frame.

**11 · THE SPEAR PIERCE — HERO**
Azulon compresses to a **single folded white dart** of pure flame and dives straight
through the mass. A thin, perfect line of white light punches through the black body —
in one side, out the other.

**12 · ANNIHILATION AND ASCENSION — BOTH**
White fire **erupts from inside** the Stain. The tar burns to vapour, the paper body
crumbles outward into a slow cloud of glowing dust, and clean white cranes rise through
the wreckage. Azulon lands, wings folding, edges still smoking white. The void is empty.

## Production notes

- **No identity bleed.** Two-figure shots keep `image1 = A_bust.png` so the *hero* face
  anchors the render, and the Stain is described as the second/distant figure. Monster-
  only shots use `image1 = seraph_ref.png` and carry **no** Azulon refs.
- **Density.** Never a lone figure in empty space — every frame carries drifting pages,
  script fragments, goo strands, embers, or dust.
- **Runtime target.** 12 shots × ~5 s ≈ **60 s** fight film.
- **Deterministic seeds** 800–811 for reproducibility.
