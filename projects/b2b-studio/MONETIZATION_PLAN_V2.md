# Monetization plan v2: all business lines and named prospects (2026-11-24)

Supersedes the target lists in `MONETIZATION_DEEP_ANALYSIS.md` and `FOUNDER_FRAMEWORKS.md`; the economics, pricing tiers and gates there still apply. Labels: **M** measured, **E** estimate to validate, **V** verify before use. Prices in EUR, ex-VAT.

## 1. What changed since v1

- **Production capability proven** (M): LTX-2.5 at ~$0.04/clip; MiniMax-H3 self-hosted at ~$0.30/clip (6.8 min/clip on 2×A100, 768p, native ambient audio); Gemini 3 Pro Image keyframes with reference images keep people, products and places consistent across shots; Gemini TTS voice-overs in Macedonian, Serbian and Albanian, verified by automatic transcription.
- **New formats:** 5-shot story commercials (hook → promise → proof → people → payoff, one hero, one place, local-language VO) and **timelapse commercials** (locked camera, start+end keyframes per stage).
- **New named prospects:** Foton Macedonia (with a two-step pitch and a lease-for-content barter), Vincinni/Makprogres (two commercials built from their real packs, logo and factory), and 10 non-EU Balkan growth companies.
- **Voice decision:** OmniVoice rejected (poor quality; weights CC-BY-NC). Gemini voices for client work.

## 3. Strategy per line

### A1. Construction and developers
- **Offer:** a 25-second "From empty plot to your keys" timelapse built from *their* architect renders and site photos, plus a 5-shot off-plan story ad (Concord-style).
- **Why they buy:** they sell before the building exists; drones can't film the future.
- **Targets:** Granit, Adora, Limak, East Gate, Stenton, Zikol, Krmzov-MR, Turmak (MK); Gener 2, Concord/Kastrati (AL); Millennium Team, Beograd na vodi (RS).
- **Hook:** a free 6-second spec clip from one of their published renders, shown in the first meeting.
- **Upsell:** one clip per construction milestone (monthly retainer while the project sells).

### A2. Car and truck dealers (Foton Macedonia first)
- **Assets ready:** 21-clip showreel, 6-shot V9 specs film (engine, 48V hybrid, 129 kW / 450+50 Nm, 4×4 lock, cabin, €42,900 / 5-year warranty), Macedonian two-step pitch (`FOTON_PITCH_MK.md`).
- **Sequence:** Pilot €600–900 → Launch €1,800–3,500 → monthly content; at month 2–3 offer the **G7 lease-for-content barter** (12 months, 60-day exit). Ownership swap only after 6–12 months of results.
- **Scale-out:** ~30 importers in Skopje (Chinese brands entering the market need content most); reuse the specs-film template per model.

### A3. FMCG / confectionery (Vincinni first)
- **Assets ready:** two 5-shot commercials ("Мали моменти, голема радост" with their slogan "Секогаш по твој вкус"; factory film ending on "It's Good. It's Vincinni.") built from their real packs, logo and factory look (`VINCINNI_VIDEO_STUDY.md`).
- **Offer:** campaign packs per product launch (they relaunch packaging often: Almi, Wafreta, Premier, Strudla), seasonal (Christmas, Ramadan, back-to-school), and multi-language versions for their 55–60 export markets (MK/SR/SQ first).
- **Licence routing:** export markets include the EU → LTX versions for anything shown in the EU; MiniMax-H3 versions only for domestic/non-EU display.
- **Next FMCG targets:** Zito Luks, Vitaminka, Pelisterka, Tikves, Bimilk/dairies (MK); Bingo, Gomex (regional retail-food).

### A4. Real-estate agencies and staging
- **Offer:** per-layout staging timelapse (empty → furnished → styled) from the agency's own photos; volume pricing (5 layouts €1,500).
- **Channel:** agencies listed in the research (LEVEL, My Property, Astoria, REM, Domik…) and developers' sales offices.

### B-lines (pitch after the first A reference)
- **Interior studios, renovation, kitchen/bath, landscaping, solar, shop fit-out, exhibition stands, road builders:** same timelapse engine, new keyframes per client; sell as "your project, sped up" for portfolios and social.
- **Retail chains (KAM, Viva Fresh):** opening films and monthly promo spots; recruitment for new stores.
- **Pharma/industrial:** through 2–3 Skopje ad agencies on white-label (they hold the accounts); employer-branding films are the entry point.

## 7. Risks specific to the new lines
- **Brand accuracy (dealers, FMCG):** generated products drift; always build from official pack/vehicle images and get keyframe approval before rendering.
- **Trademark and publication:** nothing with their logo goes public without written approval.
- **Barter tax:** two invoices with VAT; set up by an accountant.
- **Client concentration:** keep any one client under a third of working time.
- **Licence:** MiniMax-H3 output never shown in the EU/UK/US/KR.
