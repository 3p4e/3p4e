# High-growth companies in the non-EU Western Balkans: prospects for a Skopje AI-video studio

Research date: 2026-11-23. Scope: Serbia, North Macedonia, Albania, Kosovo, Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
Purpose: prospect list for brand films, product videos, recruitment videos and construction/real-estate visualisations.

## How to read this

- **Confidence**
  - ✅ Official source: a filing, the stock exchange, the company's own annual report or an IFI press release.
  - ⚠️ Secondary press. Most rankings here (Biznis.rs "99 najuspešnijih", Club 200, Monitor) are built from registry filings but published by the press.
  - ❓ Unverified, or a third-party estimate.
- **Growth index** (Biznis.rs tables): revenue in year N divided by revenue in year N-1. For example, 1.38 means +38%.
- **Non-EU flag**
  - 🟢 Sells mainly outside the EU/UK/US/KR. Output shown to its audience would mostly stay in non-EU markets.
  - 🟡 Mixed.
  - 🔴 Mainly EU/US customers, so output from a model that bans display in the EU/UK/US/Korea is risky.
- **Websites:** a website is listed only if I saw it in a source. "Not checked" means a likely domain was not opened, so do not use it blindly.
- **Reporting years:** many rankings report 2023 data, published in 2024. The year is given in each row.
- **Not covered:** contact persons were not collected. This file contains professional public business information only.

## Top-10 shortlist: why each is a strong video prospect

1. **Beograd na vodi (Belgrade Waterfront), Serbia.** Revenue +38% in 2024 and RSD 7.5 bn profit. The flagship real-estate project in the region needs a constant flow of tower and amenity visualisations and launch films, and sells to domestic and diaspora buyers.
2. **Gener 2 / Gjonaj Holding, Albania.** Revenue +73% in 2024, the largest Albanian builder. Its mix of infrastructure, residential and energy fits construction-progress films, project visualisations and recruitment content in a tight labour market.
3. **Concord Investment and Kastrati Construction, Albania.** +374% and +57.5% in 2024. Residential and hotel developers selling off-plan in a booming Tirana market, which is the core use case for real-estate visualisation.
4. **KAM DOO plus KAM Imobilien, North Macedonia (Skopje, a local client).** Revenue up about 78% from 2020 to 2024, new stores every year and about 1,400 staff. KAM Imobilien adds K Mall leasing. Fits product and promo videos, store openings and recruitment, all aimed at the domestic (non-EU) market.
5. **Viva Fresh, Kosovo.** About €393 m turnover (+19%), regional expansion via the Spar Albania deal (EBRD €25 m). Needs rebrand and launch content for Albania and North Macedonia, plus recruitment for new stores; all target markets are non-EU.
6. **Alkaloid AD, Skopje.** €309 m sales, exports +14%, 3,000+ staff, 253 new jobs in 2024. A local blue-chip with corporate film, employer-branding and product-explainer needs. 68% of sales are exported and Western Europe is 22% of sales, so keep EU-facing output on unrestricted models.
7. **Replek AD, Skopje.** Sales tripled in 4–5 years (+23–24% in 2024), aiming for 40 export markets. Needs export-market brand and product films; check its market split first.
8. **Millennium Team, Serbia.** Revenue +34% in 2024, profit up about 15x. A large contractor and developer (Hotel Jugoslavija towers) with heavy recruitment needs and project-visualisation demand.
9. **Nelt Group, Serbia.** €1.4–1.5 bn group, growing 8–9% a year, with African and Balkan markets that are almost all non-EU. Fits recruitment, corporate and trade-marketing videos for brand principals.
10. **SPEEEX, Kosovo.** About 1,560 staff and an EBRD-backed Swiss acquisition. A heavy hirer, so recruitment and employer-branding videos for a Kosovo/Albanian audience are an ideal non-EU use. Its corporate sales content for DACH must use EU-safe models.

Runners-up:
- Bingo (BiH retail and food, KM 2.18 bn)
- BALFIN / Neptun (Albania; regional retail and real estate)
- Gomex (Serbia retail; 250+ stores)
- Hotels Group Montenegro Stars (tourism)
- RES (North Macedonia energy; revenue up more than 23x)
- Adora Engineering (Skopje developer with Greek and Serbian projects)

## Licence note (output banned in the EU/UK/US/Korea)

- **Safest (🟢):** domestic-market retailers, real-estate developers and contractors. These are KAM, Viva Fresh, Bingo, Gomex, Voli, Laković, Gener 2, Concord, Kastrati, Millennium Team and Beograd na vodi. Developers can still market to diaspora buyers living in the EU, so geo-restrict distribution where needed.
- **Avoid the restricted model (🔴):** IT exporters selling to the US and EU (HTEC, Typhoon HIL, Softech) and SPEEEX's DACH sales material.
