# Волшебната книга — Епизода 1: „Фенрир, волкот од Асгард“ (v2)

**Format:** 2D painted storybook · ages 3–6 · Macedonian narration · target length **≈ 6:20**
**Narrator:** a warm female storyteller (Gemini **Leda**).
**Rule for v2:** **every event on the book's page is in the episode.** The table at the end maps each one to its segment. The narration is in our own words; the book's sentences and illustrations are not copied.
**Hard moments** (the iron chain breaking, the cry, Tyr's hand) are **said** in the narration but **never shown**. The camera turns away to the sky.

---

## Characters (for the reference sheet)

| Character | Look |
|---|---|
| **ФЕНРИР** | Grey wolf, big amber eyes, fluffy tail. A puppy at first, later as big as a house. He never shows sharp teeth. |
| **ЛОКИ** | Slim, with a green cloak and a cheeky but kind smile. A trickster god, never a villain. |
| **ЈОРМУНГАНДИР** | A long sea serpent, green-blue, with round eyes. Funny-strange, not scary. |
| **ХЕЛ** | A pale, quiet child in a long dark cloak, half her face in shadow. Mysterious, not scary. |
| **ОДИН** | An old grandfather with a long white beard, a wide-brimmed hat, a blue cloak and two ravens. |
| **ТИР** | A young god with a short red beard, a blue tunic and a round shield. Kind and brave. |
| **ЏУЏИЊАТА** | Three small blacksmiths with braided beards, leather aprons and hammers, working in a glowing cave. |
| **ТЕНКИОТ СИНЏИР** | A very thin, silvery-glowing chain that looks like silk. |
| **ВОЛШЕБНАТА КНИГА** | A large old book with a golden clasp. Its pages glow. This is the series' opening and closing device. |

**Style prompt:** *2D hand-painted children's storybook illustration, soft watercolor and gouache textures, warm rounded shapes, gentle lighting, cozy and friendly, no scary elements, no gore, no text.*

**Small differences from the book (nothing removed):**
- The book says Loki had "three sons". In the myth, Hel is a girl, so the script says **"три деца"**.
- The sea and the misty land in segment 06 are added from the myth, to show where the two went.
- Seg. 02, 15, 19, 20 and 21 are our own framing: the hook, the question to the children, the moral and the goodbye.
