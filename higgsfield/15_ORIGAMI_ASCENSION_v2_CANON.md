# The Origami Ascension — v2 canon (corrected story)

Supersedes the BLOCK 3 story in `02_PRODUCTION_PROMPTS.md` (lines 207-270).
The v1 block made the paper seraph the HERO and the ink a separate antagonist.
That is now inverted, and matches what `04_REFERENCE_STYLE_AND_FILM4_v3.md` §4
originally recorded about the reference: **the paper seraph is the monster.**

## Story (B. Nikolov, 2026-11-03)

> The origami seraph is corrupted. The edges of its wings are stained with
dripping black goo. Azulon, the hero, is the seraph sent to hunt down the
corruption and destroy it.

So there is **one** celestial body, not two species. A seraph of folded
parchment — once immaculate — has been infected. The corruption enters at the
wing edges and bleeds inward as dripping black goo. Azulon is a seraph of the
same order, sent after it.

## The two figures

### CORRUPTED SERAPH — the monster

- Immaculate folded ivory parchment where it is still clean: crisp origami
  plates, visible paper fibre, sharp creases.
- **Corruption:** the edges of every wing are stained black. The goo is wet,
  glossy, tar-like, and **drips** — long strands falling from the wing tips,
  pooling beneath, leaving dark soaked tide-lines in the paper.
- The staining spreads *inward* along the folds. Where it passes, the blackletter
  script printed on the parchment smears and bleeds.
- Six wings, hagard proportions: too many joints, too thin, wing tips drooping
  under the weight of the goo.
- No human face. The head is a blank folded paper mask, the eye-holes weeping
  black.

### AZULON — the sent seraph

- Same head canon as everywhere else in this project (see `AZULON_CANON.md`):
  completely hairless smooth shaved head, clean-shaven, no moustache/beard/
  stubble, eyebrows kept, natural grey-green eyes, very long high-elf ears,
  Selûne crescent on the forehead, unretouched photographic skin, lean wiry
  build.
- Armour and wings: immaculate folded ivory parchment, **clean** — the visual
  contrast that carries the film.
- Cold white flame along the feather edges that does not consume the paper.
- Weapon: a storm of folded paper blades and cranes edged in white light.
  **Not crystals** — crystal shards from a paper being break material logic
  (`01_REVIEW_gemini_chat.md` §e).

## Palette and light

Absolute black void. Warm ivory parchment. Cold white flame. The corruption is
matte black with an oily iridescent sheen. No warm light except the flame.

## The arc (5 beats)

1. **The monster revealed.** The corrupted seraph looms, goo dripping from
   six stained wings, pooling below.
2. **The hunter arrives.** Azulon enters the void, clean parchment and cold
   flame, and sees the stain.
3. **Contact.** The goo reaches Azulon — a wing tip or vambrace is splattered
   black. It spreads.
4. **The hunt.** Azulon re-folds the stain clean and answers with folded light.
5. **Destruction.** The corruption burns white and the monster collapses into
   drifting paper dust and clean cranes.
